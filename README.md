# 📊 Weekly Progress Tracker

A visual, interactive weekly tracker built for simplicity and daily motivation. No installation, build process, or coding knowledge required!

---

## 🚀 How to Open and Use
1. **Double-click** `start.bat` (or double-click `index.html`) in this folder.
2. It immediately opens in your default browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, etc.).

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Bar Charts**:
  - **Daily Breakdown**: Stacked bar chart showing effort per goal Monday through Sunday.
  - **Goal Target Benchmark**: Switch the filter from "All Goals" to an individual goal to see how your daily progress compares against your daily target benchmark line.
  - **Effort by Category**: Doughnut chart showing distribution across Study, Fitness, Work, Habits, etc.
  - **4-Week Progress Trend**: Compares this week's total momentum with the previous 3 weeks.
- **Weekly Score & KPI Cards**:
  - Weekly goal completion percentage ring & progress bar.
  - Total units/hours logged this week.
  - Active day tracker (Mon–Sun indicators).
  - Top focus category.
- **Interactive Check-ins & Daily Logging**:
  - Click any day cell (`Mon`, `Tue`, `Wed`, etc.) on a goal row to log or adjust your hours/units.
  - Use quick add buttons (`+0.5`, `+1`, `+2`) or type an exact amount.
- **Automatic Saving**:
  - Everything is saved directly inside your browser's local storage.
  - You can close the tab or turn off your computer anytime without losing your data.
- **Export & Import Backup**:
  - Download a JSON backup anytime or load it on another device from the settings gear icon.
- **Print / PDF Summary**:
  - Ready-to-print weekly report for physical journals or records.
- **Celebration Effects**:
  - Animated confetti fires when you complete your goals!
